Workflow Timeline Chart, 

Process flow: Requestor, then Budget, then Requestor, then Accounting Support, then Signatories, then Cashier.

Step 1, REQUESTOR
Requestor fills up the transaction submission form. No attachments yet. Mandatory Documentary Requirements are not submitted at this stage.

Step 2, BUDGET OFFICER
Budget Officer scans the proposed program or project. Checks if it has a source of funds for that program or project.

Step 3, REQUESTOR
Once the Budget Officer approves and confirms allotted budget or funds, the Requestor proceeds to submit the Mandatory Documentary Requirements.

Step 4, ACCOUNTING SUPPORT
Accounting Support scans and inspects all attachments submitted.

Step 5, ACCOUNTING SUPPORT
Once all attachments are approved, Accounting Support endorses the transaction to the Signatory Personnel(s) for Document Approval and Signature.

Step 6, CASHIER
Once approved by the Signatory Personnel(s), the transaction proceeds to the Cashier for Release of Payment.
